# SmartBudget Manager

An Excel workbook that helps you record your monthly income and expenses, see your balance automatically, and get a warning when you go over budget.

## What it does

- Records income and expenses (date, description, type, category, amount)
- Sorts expenses into categories: Food, Transport, Rent, Health, Education
- Calculates total income, total expenses and the monthly balance automatically
- Shows a pie chart of expenses by category
- Shows **WITHIN BUDGET** (green) or **OVER BUDGET** (red) against your monthly budget

## What you need

- Microsoft Excel 2016, 2019, 2021 or Microsoft 365 (or a compatible program)
- The file `SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx`
- No internet connection or account

## Get started

1. Open `SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx`. If Excel shows **Protected View**, select **Enable Editing**.
2. Go to the **Transactions** sheet and type each income or expense on a new row. Choose **Type** and **Category** from the drop-down lists.
3. Go to the **Categories** sheet to change the monthly budget for each category.
4. Open the **Dashboard** sheet to read your balance, chart and budget status. You do not type on this sheet.
5. Save with **Ctrl + S**.
